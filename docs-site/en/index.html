Get Started in 3 Steps

Make the AI truly get you

Step 1

📦 Download required models

Tagging, face recognition, and semantic search all rely on on-device AI models. Download them in "Model Center → Required" — 7 models, ~1.5 GB.

Required≈ 1.5 GBWi-Fi recommended
Step 2

🔄 Run a scan

Open the bottom "Gallery Cleanup" tab → "Scan" and scan so PoLang learns the people and scenes in your gallery (faces + semantics), about 1.5h / 10k photos. Remember to keep background activity on.

Required~1.5h / 10k
Step 3

🧠 Understand image content

Want "beach" or "night scene" searches to be sharper? Run image understanding to tag scenes, objects, and activities — about 24h / 10k photos, best overnight while charging.

Optional~24h / 10k
Step 4

🔑 Sign up for quota

AI chat runs on a cloud-based model by default. Sign in with email for a higher free quota (guests get less). A network connection is all you need.

Cloud AI chatFree quota
Step 5

👥 Teach AI your people

Name people and set relationships (family / friends…), then add facts in "AI Memory" — PoLang gets to know you better over time.

Smarter AIPeople & relationships
Step 6

📡 Remote control (optional)

Connect Feishu / Telegram to drive your gallery from IM in natural language — results come right back to the chat. Telegram uses long-polling, no public IP needed.

FAQ

FAQ

Are my photos uploaded to the cloud?

No. Face detection, beauty, and tagging run on your device. Your photos never leave your phone.

I just installed it — why can't I search, and the AI seems unresponsive?

Smart search relies on on-device models and a one-time gallery scan. First download the required models in "Model Center" and run a scan — see the Getting Started guide.

How long does the scan take? Will it drain my battery?

The required face + semantic scan takes about 1.5h per 10k photos; the more accurate image-understanding pass takes about 24h per 10k — best run overnight while charging. Already-processed photos won't repeat.

Is AI chat paid?

Chat runs on a cloud-based model with a free quota; sign in for a higher quota. A network connection is required.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases — every feature is free.

Does it work offline?

On-device capabilities like tagging, face recognition, and scene detection work fully offline. Cloud AI chat requires a network connection.

Which devices are supported?

Android 7.0 (API 24) and above; beauty and on-device inference run best on arm64 devices. An iOS version is in development — coming soon.

Is any data collected?

No. We never collect photos or other media data, and we don't sell your data. Email / guest device identifiers are used only to count your free quota. See the Privacy Policy.
