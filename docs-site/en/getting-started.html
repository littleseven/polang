5 Steps to Make the AI Truly Get You

PoLang's smart search and AI chat rely on on-device models and a one-time gallery scan. Finish the steps below so the app can actually understand you.

📦 It all starts in Settings & the Organize tab

Model downloads, account, people, and remote channels live in the app's Settings; the scan entry is at the bottom tab "Gallery Cleanup → Scan". Each step below points you to the right screen — the UI may differ slightly in the latest version.

1Finish the onboarding guide

On first launch, the app walks you through the onboarding screens to grant gallery and other permissions — the prerequisite for all on-device recognition and search. About 1 minute.

Required~1 min
2Download required models

Tagging, face recognition, and semantic search all rely on on-device AI models. Go to Model Center → Required and download them all (7 models, ~1.5 GB). The Recommended models (matting, voice) are optional.

Required≈ 1.5 GBWi-Fi recommended
3Run a Pass 1 scan

Let the app learn the people and scenes in your gallery. Go to the bottom tab Gallery Cleanup → Scan and start scanning (face detection + semantic encoding, ~1.5h / 10k photos). Remember to enable background activity when prompted, or it may be paused in the background. The UI may differ slightly in the latest version.

Required~1.5h / 10k
4Understand image content

Run Image Understanding to generate scene, object, and activity tags for every photo — it makes searches like "beach", "night scene", or "family picnic" sharper. ~24h / 10k, best overnight while charging. Pause anytime — already-processed photos won't repeat.

Required~24h / 10k
5(Optional) Sign up and teach AI your people

Sign in with email for a higher free quota (guests get less). After scanning, people are detected automatically — name each person and set relationships (family / friends / colleagues…), add facts in AI Memory, and connect Feishu / Telegram to drive your gallery from IM. PoLang gets smarter in every conversation.

OptionalFree quotaPeople & relationshipsFeishu / Telegram
